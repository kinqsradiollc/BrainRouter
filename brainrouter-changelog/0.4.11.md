# 0.4.11 — Worktree isolation that merges back, a self-documenting config, and memory hygiene

0.4.11 turns spawned-agent worktree isolation into a **safe default** — isolated
children now merge their work back onto your tree instead of leaving it stranded
— relocates worktrees under your BrainRouter home, makes `config.json`
**self-documenting**, and adds two **memory-accuracy** tools. All published at
0.4.11.

## Multi-agent worktree isolation

- **Merge-back, on by default.** `cli.childWorkspaceIsolation` now defaults to
  **`auto`**: a spawned write/shell child works in its own detached `git
  worktree`, and on **clean completion** its changes are `git apply --check`-gated
  and merged onto your working tree. A conflicting or drifted patch is **preserved
  as a recovery patch** (never smeared into your tree), and a failed child's work
  stays isolated. Non-git workspaces and read-only children share the parent root,
  so the new default is safe everywhere.
- **It tells you what happened.** The child-completion notice now reports the
  merge outcome — `✓ N file(s) merged into your tree`, or `⚠ NOT merged — recover
  with /agents diff <id>` — surfaced on `wait_agent` / `list_agents` too. (As a
  side effect this fixed a latent bug where an auto-chained reviewer read the
  *pre-merge* tree.)
- **`/agents diff <id>`** — review, **apply**, or **discard** the recovery patch
  captured from a child's worktree. `apply` is `git apply --check`-gated and runs
  from the child's repo root.
- **Worktrees moved out of `$TMPDIR`.** They now live under
  **`~/.brainrouter/worktrees/<repo>/<child>`** (realpath-resolved), which fixes a
  macOS `/var` → `/private/var` symlink-drift bug that could make a worktree path
  "escape the workspace root." Relocate them anywhere with the new
  **`cli.worktreeRoot`** knob.
- **Recovery-patch GC.** Stale worktree-recovery patches are pruned on a 7-day
  retention window (wired into the session reconcile).

## Configuration

- **`config.json` self-hydrates.** On launch, every *safe* `cli.*` knob's default
  is written into your `config.json`, so all settings are **visible and editable**
  (and a newly-added knob appears on the next launch). The preference-layered
  knobs (`theme` / `effort` / `quiet`) are deliberately left out so `/theme`,
  `/effort`, and `/quiet` stay dynamic; your overrides are never touched.
- **`cli.worktreeRoot`** — relocate child worktrees onto a fast/scratch disk or
  outside backups.

## Memory accuracy

- **`/memory verify` (+ `memory_verify` MCP tool).** Reconcile code-anchored
  memories against the current source index: each is classified **fresh**,
  **re-anchorable** (its file changed — a reindex can refresh it), or
  **archivable** (its source is gone). Read-only by default; `--apply` archives
  *only* the confirmed-dead anchors (recoverable expiry, never the merely-stale).
- **Churn-weighted decay (MEM-CHURN).** Memories anchored to **high-churn** source
  files now age out faster. The churn signal (`git log --since=90d` per file) is
  captured at index time and folded into recall's half-life. Null-safe by
  construction — files with no captured churn (and every non-code memory) score
  exactly as before.

## Also in 0.4.11

- **Auto-chain decision.** `/auto-chain` (review/verify after every worker) stays
  **off by default** — friendlier to rate-limited providers — and is enabled
  per-workspace with `/auto-chain review|both`.
- **Design specs** for two upcoming features landed under `docs/specs/`:
  per-session isolation (two terminals on one repo) and the build-loop workflow
  (plan → implement → verify → review).
