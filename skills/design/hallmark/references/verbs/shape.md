# `/design shape` — from brief to concept

**Edits the target:** no · **Runs `design_detect` first:** no · **Mode:** from `--mode` or inferred (see [`../modes.md`](../modes.md))

## Purpose

Turn a brief into a shaped concept before anything is built: the macrostructure, the archetypes for nav and footer, the world, the type direction, the one asymmetry, and the trade-offs you made. Shape is thinking on paper; the default build executes it.

## Inputs

- the brief (words, a doc, or a `product.md`)
- `design.md` if the project already has a system (then shape stays inside it)
- the mode

## Steps

1. Restate the brief in three lines: who, what they must do, how it should feel.
2. Pick the macrostructure and say why the obvious one was rejected.
3. Pick the world (`../genres/worlds.md`) and the type direction; name the accent's one job.
4. Sketch the section outline with the question each section answers.
5. List the trade-offs and the two alternatives you did not choose.
6. Hand off: the default Design workflow builds from this shape; diversification is already satisfied by the choices here.

## Output contract

A one-page concept: brief restated · macrostructure · world · type · accent job · section outline · trade-offs · alternatives. No code.

## Do not

- Do not build.
- Do not offer three concepts when one was asked for; offer one and name the alternatives.
- Do not pick the template structure by default.
