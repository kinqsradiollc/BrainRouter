# `/design bolder` — a stronger point of view

**Edits the target:** yes · **Runs `design_detect` first:** yes · **Mode:** from `--mode` or inferred (see [`../modes.md`](../modes.md))

## Purpose

Raise the page's confidence: bigger type where it matters, higher contrast, one deliberate asymmetry, a stronger hero or opening, less hedging in the copy. Bolder is not louder — it removes timidity, it does not add noise.

## Inputs

- the target
- `design.md` if present — bolder moves within its accent and type roles

## Steps

1. Find the one thing the page is about and make it unmistakably first: scale, placement, contrast.
2. Increase type contrast between levels (display vs body) rather than making everything larger.
3. Introduce one asymmetry — an offset column, a bleed, a large numeral — and stop at one.
4. Cut hedging copy ('may', 'can help', 'solutions') for direct statements the product can back.
5. Keep the palette; use the existing accent with more conviction rather than adding colours.
6. Re-run `design_detect`; the count is equal or lower.

## Output contract

What was made first, the one asymmetry you chose, the copy you sharpened. Contrast values for anything you changed.

## Do not

- Do not add gradients, glows, or effects as a stand-in for confidence.
- Do not invent claims to sound bold.
- Do not break the grid more than once.
